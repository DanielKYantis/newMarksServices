01

Overview

When you contact Mark's Services, you may provide information needed to review and respond to a home-service request.

This policy describes the information the website may collect, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

02

Information We Collect

The website may process information you submit and limited technical information generated when the site is used.

Information You Provide

When sending a service request, you may provide:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Property service area and preferred timing
  • Project descriptions and photos if requested
  • Communication preferences

Technical Information

The website or analytics tools may collect limited technical data such as:

  • Device and browser type
  • Pages viewed and general interaction data
  • Approximate location derived from technical data when available
  • Browser language and basic settings
03

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

Review and respond to service requests
Discuss project scope and scheduling
Send requested communications
Protect the website and prevent misuse
Understand and improve website performance
04

Sharing and Disclosure

Information is not sold. It may be shared only as reasonably needed for the purposes described below.

With Your Direction

Information may be shared when you request or authorize coordination with a licensed trade, manufacturer, utility, HOA, or other project-related party.

For Legal or Safety Reasons

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with law or valid legal process
  • Enforce website and service terms
  • Detect or address fraud, abuse, or security issues
  • Protect users, property, and legal rights
05

Security Practices

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect submitted information. No website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Encrypted website connections where supported
Limited access to submitted service-request data
Reasonable retention and handling practices
06

Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to:

Access information you submitted
Correct inaccurate contact or project details
Request deletion when legally and operationally appropriate
Limit certain communications or processing
07

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the website, analytics, or service-request process changes. The effective date will be revised when updates are posted.

Continued use of the website after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
